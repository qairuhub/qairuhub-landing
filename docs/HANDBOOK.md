## What QairuHub is

### QairuHub in one paragraph

QairuHub is a Kazakhstan tech community, born at Qazaq AI Research University (QAIRU) in Astana. Everyone is welcome at any skill level. Students run it, and it exists so that every student who wants to build something gets three things: a team, practical knowledge and a stage.

QairuHub runs events, hackathons, hackathon preparation, mentorship and masterclasses. It works with the ecosystem around it and builds real products. People with domain knowledge share it, create projects and build teams with developers they find inside the university on the community platform, community.qairuhub.com. QairuHub also runs QairuHub Accelerator and QairuHub Demo Day: projects built with the community's resources, mentors and teams get a stage in front of students, faculty, professors, university partners and investors. Both are planned, and their exact dates are to be announced.

The tagline is "Learn it. Build it. Launch it." The long-term goal, in the team's own words, is to grow QAIRU into the strongest tech community in Kazakhstan.

### Why QairuHub exists

QAIRU students build an AI project as part of their degree, and doing that alone is hard. The Club Fair deck summed up the usual problems. You have an idea but no team. You have a skill but nowhere to use it. You have a project nobody has seen. You have a question and nobody to ask.

QairuHub was set up to close that gap. The founders' framing is that compute is not the bottleneck for a QAIRU student; finishing is. The university trains specialists, and institutions like Astana Hub and Alem.ai work with people who are already building. QairuHub is the step in between. It takes a student from "I want to build something" to a person with a team, a working demo and a place to show it.

Three short lines from the founding materials describe the approach:
- A community, not a hobby circle.
- Projects, not lectures.
- Peer-to-peer, not teacher and student.

### What QairuHub is not

Being clear about what QairuHub is not helps explain what it is.

- **Not a startup or a company.** QairuHub is a non-profit student club. It is not a registered legal entity, and it holds no money and has no bank account.
- **Not a hobby club or a second student council.** The focus is shipping real projects.
- **Not run by the university.** Students run QairuHub. The team does work closely with QAIRU administration on shared goals, for example preparing students for HackAlem AI.
- **Not a competitor to Astana Hub or Alem.ai.** QairuHub serves every student who wants to build and helps them get ready for the ecosystem those organisations are part of.
- **Not owned by anyone.** No member, including the President, owns QairuHub. There is no equity and no permanent founder title.

### Where QairuHub is based

QairuHub was founded at Qazaq AI Research University (QAIRU) in Astana, Kazakhstan. QAIRU took its first students on 3 September 2026. Teaching is in English, and each year's intake is about 400 bachelor's and 100 master's students. The curriculum builds one AI project over the course of the degree.

The QAIRU campus is on the EXPO site in Astana, next door to Astana Hub, the city's tech park, and the Alem.ai cluster. That neighbourhood is part of why QairuHub exists: the ecosystem is close by, and QairuHub helps students become ready to work with it.

The community was founded on 7 September 2026, four days after QAIRU opened its doors.

## Who QairuHub is for

### Students at any skill level

QairuHub is for anyone who wants to build, whatever their experience. The founding deck puts it simply: if you know ML, come and lead; if you know nothing but want to learn, come and learn. No experience is needed, only the will to build.

Every project needs more than code. Teams need people for:
- backend and frontend
- data and machine learning
- design
- hardware and robotics
- research
- product
- media, community and events

Some parts of QairuHub are open to everyone, and one part is limited to QAIRU students for now:
- **Open to all:** events and the Telegram channel.
- **QAIRU students only:** the community platform, community.qairuhub.com, which needs a university email ending in @qairu.edu.kz.

Whether students from other universities can get platform accounts has not been decided yet (TBA).

### People with an idea, or without one

**If you have an idea**, bring it. Publish it as a project on community.qairuhub.com and open roles that describe who you need: the skill, the hours per week and a deadline. Students with matching skills can find the project through Team Finder.

**If you don't have an idea**, pick an open one. Browse projects that are looking for people and join a team whose problem interests you.

**If you just want to learn first**, come to events, mentorship sessions and masterclasses. Nobody has to have a project on day one.

QairuHub's registration form asks what you can do and whether you have a project idea (yes, no, or still thinking). It does not ask how many hours you can give. The form is there so the team can get to know you, not to filter people out.

### Mentors, speakers, partners and investors

QairuHub works with people outside the student body too:
- **Mentors** who can guide a team in a specific area, such as ML, web, mobile or marketing.
- **Speakers** who can run a talk or a masterclass.
- **Organisations** that want to support an event, a hackathon or QairuHub Demo Day.

Finance & Partnerships is QairuHub's contact for sponsors and partners. The simplest way to reach the team is the form on this site: choose "Partner or sponsor" or "Mentor or speaker". You can also write in the Telegram channel, t.me/qairuhub.

## How to join

### Join QairuHub in four steps

1. **Follow the news.** Subscribe to the Telegram channel t.me/qairuhub, where every event and launch is announced. QairuHub is also on Instagram as @qairuhub.
2. **Tell us about yourself.** Fill in the join form on this site or QairuHub's registration form. Everyone is accepted; the form helps the team understand your skills and ideas.
3. **Create a platform account.** If you are a QAIRU student, sign up at community.qairuhub.com with your @qairu.edu.kz email, then fill in your profile with skills, interests and free hours.
4. **Show up and build.** Come to the next event, join a project with an open role, or publish your own.

Joining is free. There are no fees, now or later.

community.qairuhub.com is QairuHub's community platform for people, projects and events.

### Membership levels: member, active member, core team

QairuHub has three levels, and there are no junior, middle or senior labels.

1. **Member.** Anyone who joins the community, comes to events and takes part in projects.
2. **Active member.** Someone who helps run and build QairuHub itself: events, media, the platform, partnerships. Active members join through intake waves, and the next wave will be announced on the channel (TBA). Showing real activity, such as being on a project team, giving a demo or running a session, is what gets noticed.
3. **Core team.** The elected executive board that runs the organisation day to day.

The first core team and active members were the 15 founders. Seats are not permanent: they rotate by vote, and anyone can be replaced.

### What QairuHub costs

Nothing. QairuHub is free, with no membership fees, ever. "Free, no dues, ever" is one of the founding principles.

A few more commitments sit alongside it:
- **You pay nothing.** Students are never charged to attend events, join projects or use the platform.
- **The club holds no money.** QairuHub is a non-profit student club with no bank account. When something needs funding, such as an event or prizes, the money is raised for that specific thing.
- **The club never holds investment money.** Any investment goes to the team that earned it.
- **Your project stays yours.** QairuHub takes no equity and no cut of your project's revenue.

## Programs and formats

### Events, meetups and workshops

QairuHub runs in-person events where people learn, meet and form teams. Formats include:
- **Workshops and masterclasses:** hands-on sessions on a tool or skill.
- **Meetups and talks:** people who know a domain show others what actually works.

Events are listed on community.qairuhub.com with a capacity, a list of who is going and Telegram reminders a day and an hour before. QairuHub events are announced on t.me/qairuhub.

## The platform: community.qairuhub.com

### What the platform is

community.qairuhub.com is QairuHub's community platform. In its own words, it puts people, projects and events at QAIRU in one place: who is building what, who needs people, and where to show up this week, without hundred-person chats or lost links.

- **Who built it:** QairuHub's student dev team.
- **Status:** a first version (MVP) was deployed in mid-September 2026 (Confirmed). More features are planned in the following weeks.

**What you can do:**
- Build a profile around your skills and free hours.
- Find a project with an open role, or find people for your team (Team Finder).
- Publish a project and recruit.
- Join events, with capacity limits and Telegram reminders.
- Browse the student directory, communities, clubs and shared resources.

**Access:** events and the Telegram channel are open to everyone. The platform needs an @qairu.edu.kz email for now, and an administrator approves each account. Two pages are public without an account: the project showcase (community.qairuhub.com/showcase) and the clubs directory (community.qairuhub.com/clubs).

**Languages:** English, Russian and Kazakh. The Kazakh translation is still being completed.

### Team Finder and open roles

Team Finder on community.qairuhub.com is how people and projects find each other. It has two modes.

**Find a project** lists open roles across projects. Each role says:
- the role title
- the skill it needs
- hours per week (1 to 40)
- a deadline

You can filter by project status, technology, skill and maximum weekly hours, then apply without a long "are you still looking?" chat.

**Find people** lists students by:
- program and year
- skills and interests
- hours per week and languages
- whether they are "open to projects" or "open to collaboration"

Found someone? Follow them or message them on Telegram. The platform has no internal direct messages by design; contact happens in Telegram, where students already talk.

There is no automatic team matching. People choose people.

### Platform rules

community.qairuhub.com runs on a few simple rules:

1. **University email only.** Only students with an @qairu.edu.kz email can sign in.
2. **Contact happens in Telegram.** There are no internal direct messages; profiles and clubs link to Telegram.
3. **Student-run.** Students run the platform; the university does not moderate it.
4. **Your data stays yours.** Your profile is visible to campus students or only to you, and nothing is sold.

**The community code of conduct:**
- Harassment, discrimination or intimidation is not tolerated, in private or in public.
- Nobody takes credit for other people's work, and the author is always named.
- QairuHub's name, channels and partners are not used for personal deals without core team approval.

## Values and principles

### QairuHub's principles

These principles were presented at the QAIRU Club Fair on 9 September 2026 and guide how QairuHub works:

1. **Builders over talkers.** QairuHub counts teams and demos, not people in a chat.
2. **Open by default.** Projects live in the open, in the GitHub organisation, and decisions are written down.
3. **The project is yours.** Code, rights and commercialisation are 100% yours; 0% goes to the club.
4. **Free.** No dues, ever.
5. **Peer-to-peer.** Today you learn; next semester you lead.
6. **Honest.** A direction that shows no results gets closed, not left hanging on paper.

Two more ideas come from the founding materials:
- **One owner per initiative.** Every task and project has a named person responsible for it.
- **Leave it better than you found it.** Write handover notes so the next team can continue.

<!-- kw: values, principles, culture, builders over talkers, open by default, honest, peer to peer; құндылықтар, қағидаттар; ценности, принципы, культура -->

### The four promises

At the founding session on 7 September 2026, QairuHub's founders decided four things that are not up for negotiation:

1. **Nobody owns QairuHub.** There is no equity and no permanent "founder" title. Leadership passes by vote.
2. **Members own 100% of what they build.** QairuHub makes no claim on your code or ideas and takes no share of revenue.
3. **QairuHub is a non-profit club that holds no money.** There is no bank account. Money is raised for a specific purpose, and students are never charged.
4. **Anyone can be replaced, including the President.**

These promises exist so students, partners and investors can trust that QairuHub is here to help teams, not to take from them.

QairuHub decided early which numbers matter:

### Privacy on this site
