> Having an idea is easy. **Finishing it is hard.**<br>
> QairuHub gives everyone who wants to build three things: a team, practical knowledge and a stage.

QairuHub was founded on 7 September 2026 by 15 students at Qazaq AI Research University (QAIRU), on the EXPO campus in Astana, next door to Astana Hub and the Alem.ai cluster. It's student-run and non-profit: nobody owns it, leaders are elected by vote, and anyone can be replaced.

## New here? Start in 4 steps

1. **Follow [t.me/qairuhub](https://t.me/qairuhub).** Every event and launch is announced there.
2. **Tell us about yourself** in the [join form](https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev/#join). Everyone is accepted.
3. **QAIRU student? Create your profile** on [community.qairuhub.com](https://community.qairuhub.com/sign-up) with your @qairu.edu.kz email. An admin approves each account. No university email yet? Start with the channel and events.
4. **Show up and build.** Come to the next event, join a project, or ask **Q**, the snail on [our site](https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev).

No experience needed. Not a coder? Teams also need design, research, product and media. Not at QAIRU? Events and the channel are open to everyone. Free, always.

## What QairuHub does

- **Events & masterclasses.** Workshops, meetups and hands-on sessions from people who know a domain.
- **Hackathons & preparation.** We help you register, find teammates and get ready. A QairuHub Hack Day is planned.
- **Mentorship.** Peer-to-peer: today you learn, next semester you lead.
- **Teams & projects.** Publish an idea on the platform, open roles and build with developers from your university.
- **QairuHub Demo Day.** The stage for projects built in the community: students, faculty, professors, university partners and investors. Planned; dates to be announced.
- **QairuHub Accelerator.** For teams that already have a demo. Draft plan: 8–10 weeks, mentors from week one, and QairuHub takes no share of your project (zero equity). Planned; dates to be announced.

## Build under QairuHub

**Idea → Team → Code on GitHub → Build → Demo → Featured**

1. **Publish your idea** on [community.qairuhub.com](https://community.qairuhub.com) with a one-line promise (what changes if this works?), a real status and open roles.
2. **Find teammates** in Team Finder, at events or in the channel's discussion chat.
3. **Put your code on GitHub.** Your own account is fine. Add the repo link to your project on the platform so it can appear in the [public showcase](https://community.qairuhub.com/showcase). **Want it inside the qairuhub org?** Write in the discussion chat under any post on [t.me/qairuhub](https://t.me/qairuhub) and say which project it is and your GitHub username. The CTO handles GitHub access. The exact steps are to be announced. Your project stays 100% yours either way.
4. **Build in the open.** Keep your tasks and changes on GitHub, and keep your status on the platform up to date.
5. **Demo** at a QairuHub demo session or at QairuHub Demo Day (planned, dates to be announced).
6. **Get featured.** Tell the team through the [join form](https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev/#join) ("I have a project"). The media team picks projects for our site, Telegram and Instagram, credited to your team. [How to get featured](https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev/handbook#getting-your-project-featured-on-this-site)

### Rules for new repos in this org

These rules are for new repos inside the qairuhub org. **New to GitHub?** A **repo** is a project folder on GitHub. An **issue** is a task. A **branch** is your own copy to work on. A **pull request (PR)** asks the team to add your changes to `main`, the main version.

- **One issue, one owner.** Unassigned means nobody is doing it.
- **Every change is a pull request** that someone else on the team checks and approves. `main` only gets merged work. Working alone? Ask for a reviewer in the channel's discussion chat.
- **Branch names start with** `feature/`, `hotfix/` or `docs/`. Existing repos may keep their own prefixes, such as `proposal/` in qairuhub-web.
- **Credit the author.** Always.
- **Never share members' personal data.**

## How we work

- **Builders over talkers.** We count teams and demos, not people in a chat.
- **Open by default.** Projects live in the open on GitHub, ideally in this org. Decisions get written down.
- **Your project is yours.** Code, rights and any money it earns: 100% yours, 0% to the club.
- **Free.** No dues. Ever.
- **Peer-to-peer.** Today you learn, next semester you lead.
- **Honest.** A direction with no results gets closed, not left hanging.

### Code of conduct

- No harassment, discrimination or intimidation.
- The author is always named. Nobody takes credit for other people's work.
- No personal deals under QairuHub's name without core team approval.
- Members' personal data stays inside the community.

Reports go to the CTO or the President and are handled within a week.

## FAQ for students

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need experience?</b></summary>
<br>
No. Come to events and mentorship sessions, learn with peers, and join a project in a role that fits you: code, design, research, product or media. Nobody expects a project on day one.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it free?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. No fees, now or later. QairuHub is a non-profit student club and never charges students.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I join if I'm not from QAIRU?</b></summary>
<br>
Events and the Telegram channel are open to everyone. The platform needs an @qairu.edu.kz email for now. Platform access for students from other universities is to be announced.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Who owns my project?</b></summary>
<br>
You and your team, 100%: code, rights and commercialisation. QairuHub takes no equity and makes no IP claim. Your code doesn't have to be open source, but a public repo lets your project appear in the platform's showcase.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I get my project on our website or into Demo Day?</b></summary>
<br>
Publish it on community.qairuhub.com, keep its status real, show a demo at a QairuHub session, and tell the team through the <a href="https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev/#join">join form</a>. Student projects get featured, not just the core team's. For a demo slot, talk to Community & Events. Demo Day is planned, and its dates are to be announced.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do I ask questions?</b></summary>
<br>
Ask Q on <a href="https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev">our site</a>, read the <a href="https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev/handbook">Handbook</a>, or write in the discussion chat of <a href="https://t.me/qairuhub">t.me/qairuhub</a>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I get access to the org on GitHub?</b></summary>
<br>
Ask the CTO, who handles access and technical questions, through <a href="https://t.me/qairuhub">t.me/qairuhub</a> or the <a href="https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev/#join">join form</a>. Say which project you work on and share your GitHub username. A step-by-step process for org membership is to be announced.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What is Q?</b></summary>
<br>
Q is the snail in the corner of <a href="https://qairuhub-landing.pages.dev">our site</a>, drawn from the "Q" in our logo. It answers questions about QairuHub in English or Kazakh, using the Handbook, and says so when something isn't announced yet. Snails are slow, but they always finish the journey. Check dates on the channel, and never share passwords or personal data with Q.
</details>
