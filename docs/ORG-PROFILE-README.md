## What we do

- **Events & masterclasses.** Workshops, meetups and hands-on sessions, starting with a Hackathon Mentorship on 15 September 2026.
- **Hackathons & prep.** We get teams hackathon-ready, and a QairuHub Hack Day is in the works.
- **Mentorship.** Peer-to-peer: learn this semester, lead the next.
- **Teams & projects.** Publish an idea on [community.qairuhub.com](https://community.qairuhub.com), open roles and recruit builders.
- **QairuHub Demo Day.** A planned stage for student projects, meant for students, faculty, partners and investors. First edition planned for November 2026.
- **QairuHub Accelerator.** For teams past the demo, with zero equity. Planned for spring 2027.

## Build under QairuHub

```text
1. Join          →  t.me/qairuhub · community.qairuhub.com (QAIRU students: @qairu.edu.kz)
2. Pick or pitch →  find an open role in Team Finder, or publish your own project
3. Ship          →  work in the open, keep the status honest: idea → in progress → demo
4. Launch        →  QairuHub Demo Day → hackathons → QairuHub Accelerator
```

**Contributing to org repos**
- One issue, one owner. Unassigned means it doesn't exist.
- Every change is a pull request with a review from someone other than the author; nobody commits to `main`.
- Branch names start with `feature/`, `hotfix/` or `docs/`.
- Credit the author, always. Don't use QairuHub's name for personal deals, and never share members' personal data.

## How we work

|   |   |
|---|---|
| **Builders over talkers** | We count teams and demos, not people in a chat. |
| **Open by default** | Projects live in this org. Decisions get written down. |
| **Your project is yours** | Code, rights and commercialisation stay 100% yours; 0% goes to the club. |
| **Free** | No dues. Ever. |
| **Peer-to-peer** | Today you learn, next semester you lead. |
| **Honest** | A direction with no results gets closed, not left hanging. |

## Where we are

We're on the EXPO campus of Qazaq AI Research University (QAIRU) in Astana, next door to Astana Hub and the Alem.ai cluster. QairuHub was founded on 7 September 2026 by 15 students. It's student-run and non-profit: nobody owns it, and anyone can be replaced.
